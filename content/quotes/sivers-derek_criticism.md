> "But whatever you choose, brace yourself because people are always going to tell you that you are wrong. It's why you need to know why you are doing what you are doing. Know it in advance. Use it as your compass and optimize your life around it. Let the other goals be secondary so that when the decision comes, you can choose the value that you already know matters to you."
> — Derek Sivers ^quote
